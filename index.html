My Cybersecurity Journey

I am a cybersecurity enthusiast focused on Security Operations Center (SOC) analysis, security monitoring, threat detection, log analysis, and incident response. Through hands-on labs and practical projects, I am developing my technical skills and building a portfolio that demonstrates my ability to investigate security events, identify suspicious activities, and understand common cyber threats. My goal is to grow into a skilled SOC Analyst and contribute to protecting systems, networks, and digital assets from cyber threats

I'm developing my technical knowledge through practical cybersecurity labs, networking exercises, and hands-on projects. My skills includes log analysis, vulnerability assessment, firewall configuration, and network traffic investigation.

I also believe in learning by doing. I document my practical work so I can explain the tools I used, the steps I followed, the results I observed, and what I learned from each exercise.

My long-term goal has always being to become a skilled cybersecurity professional who can help organizations detect, investigate, and respond to security threats.
